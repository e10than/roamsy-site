Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Roamsy helps you and your friends decide what to do. This page explains what information Roamsy collects, why, who can see it, and how to delete it. Roamsy is made by Ethan Lee. Questions: ethanjlee69@gmail.com.

Using Roamsy without an account

You can browse spots as a guest. As a guest, nothing about you is sent to us except ordinary requests to load spots and photos. Your location (if you allow it) stays on your phone and is used to show distances and center the map.

What we collect when you make an account

  • Email and password. Used to sign you in. Your password is stored scrambled by our database provider (Supabase), and we can never see it.
  • Name and username. Shown on your profile and on posts you share with your name.
  • Birthday. Used only to confirm you are 13 or older and to turn on extra protections for people under 18. It is never shown to anyone.
  • What you post. Spots, descriptions, the place and map pin you choose, and photos or videos you add.
  • What you do with other people's posts. Spots you save, people you follow, and reports or blocks you make.
  • Your answers in setup (interests, budget, who you go out with), used to pick better suggestions.

Who can see what

  • Posts you share with Everyone can be seen by anyone using Roamsy, including guests.
  • Friends-only posts are seen only by people you both follow. Only-me posts are seen only by you.
  • Anonymous posts. If you turn off "Show my name," other users see "Anonymous" and the app is never sent who wrote it. Roamsy itself still knows it's yours, so we can handle reports, blocks and account deletion.
  • Exact location. If you turn off "Show the exact spot," other people only see the city or area.
  • Your email and birthday are never shown to other users.

Location

If you allow it, Roamsy uses your location while the app is open to show distances, center the map, and fill in a spot when you post. Your current location is not saved to your account. A spot's location is saved only when you post it. Turning a map point into a place name uses Apple's location services. You can turn location off in iOS Settings at any time.

Photos and videos

Roamsy only opens the photos you pick. Photos and videos you add to a post are uploaded and stored so others who can see the post can see them. Photos on official Roamsy spots come from Wikimedia Commons (credited under each photo) or are labeled "Example photo" when they show the kind of place rather than the place itself.

People under 18

Roamsy is for people 13 and older. If you are under 18: your posts always show your name (no anonymous posts), the exact spot is always hidden, and new posts default to friends-only. If we learn that someone under 13 has an account, we delete it. Parents can email us to have a child's account removed.

Safety and moderation

You can report any post and block any person. Reported posts are reviewed by the Roamsy team, and posts reported by several people are hidden automatically. Accounts that break the rules can be suspended.

Roamsy Plus and rewards

If Roamsy Plus is offered, purchases are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details. We only store whether you have Plus and until when. Free months earned through the Founding local program are recorded on your account.

Who helps us run Roamsy

  • Supabase stores accounts, posts and photos.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website and official spot photos.
  • Apple provides maps, location services and in-app purchases.

We don't sell your information, show ads, or use tracking or advertising IDs.

Deleting your data

In the app, go to Me, then Account, then Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, posts, photos, follows, saves and reports. You can also edit or delete any single post at any time. To delete as a guest, just delete the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Big changes will also be shown in the app.
