Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Roamsy helps you save and find things to do and places to hang out. This page explains what the app does with your information. The short version: your ideas stay on your phone, and we do not run a server that collects them.

What the app stores

Everything you create in Roamsy (your ideas, likes, saved items, and settings) is saved only on your device. We do not receive a copy. If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it.

Location

If you allow it, Roamsy reads your approximate location while the app is open. It is used to show how far ideas are from you, to center the map, and to fill in the spot when you add an idea. Your location is not sent to us. To turn a point on the map into a place name, the app may use Apple's built-in location services, which are governed by Apple's privacy policy. You can turn location off at any time in iOS Settings, and the app still works.

Photos and videos

If you choose to attach a photo or video to an idea, Roamsy asks permission to open your photo library. Only the items you pick are used, and they stay on your device.

Accounts, ads, and tracking

Roamsy has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking. We do not sell or share personal information, because we do not collect it.

Sample content

The other people you see in the app (friends and their ideas) are sample content that comes with the app. They are not real users, and nothing you do is shared with them.

Children

Roamsy does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
