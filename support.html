Common questions

I didn't get the confirmation email. Check your spam folder, wait a minute, then tap "Send the email again" in the app. After tapping the link in the email, come back to Roamsy and sign in.

The map does not show where I am. Open iOS Settings, then Roamsy, then Location, and choose "While Using the App". You can also type your city in the app.

I cannot add photos. Open iOS Settings, then Roamsy, then Photos, and allow access. Free posts can have up to 3 photos.

Who can see my posts? Each post has a "Who can see it" setting: Everyone, Friends (people you both follow), or Only me. You can also turn off "Show my name" to post anonymously after your account is a week old. Under 18, your name always shows and the exact spot is hidden.

How do group picks work? On the Pick tab, start a group pick and send the code to your friends. Everyone votes yes or no, and Roamsy shows the spot you agree on.

Someone posted something bad. Open the post and tap "Report this post". You can also block whoever posted it. We review reports.

How do I delete my account? In the app go to Me, then Account, then Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, posts and photos.
