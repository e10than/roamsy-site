Common questions

The map does not show where I am. Open iOS Settings, then Roamsy, then Location, and choose "While Using the App". You can also set where you are on the Discover tab.

I cannot attach photos to an idea. Open iOS Settings, then Roamsy, then Photos, and allow access.

Who can see my ideas? Each idea has a "Who can see this" setting (Everyone, Friends, or Only me). Right now your ideas are saved on your device only, and the friends you see are sample accounts.

How do I delete my data? Delete the app. Everything is stored on your device, so it is removed with it. You can also delete any single idea from its detail screen.
